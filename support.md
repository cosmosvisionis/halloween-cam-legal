# Support — Halloween Costume AI Cam

Need help? Email **[ege.bakici35@gmail.com](mailto:ege.bakici35@gmail.com)**. We reply in English, usually within 2 business days.

## Common questions

**My costume didn't appear.**
If a costume doesn't arrive, the credit is returned automatically, usually within a few minutes. Check your connection and try again.

**How do credits work?**
Your first reveal is free and watermarked. Each costume after that uses 1 credit. Credits never expire.

**I reinstalled the App and my credits are gone.**
There are no accounts, so credits belong to this install. Email us and we will help.

**How do I stop the app from sending my photos?**
In the App, open Settings and turn off **AI photo processing**. No photo is sent until you agree again.

**What happens to my photo?**
It is sent to our AI provider, fal.ai, to make your costume. We ask fal.ai not to store it, and our server does not store it. Full details: https://cosmosvisionis.github.io/halloween-cam-legal/privacy-policy
