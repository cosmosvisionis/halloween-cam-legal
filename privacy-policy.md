# Privacy Policy — Halloween Costume AI Cam

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026
**Developer:** Egehan Bakıcı ("we," "us," "our")
**Contact:** ege.bakici35@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information Halloween Costume AI Cam (the "App") uses, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain language.

## 1. The short version

- You choose a selfie. We send it to our AI processing provider, **fal.ai**, to turn it into a Halloween costume look (skeleton, creepy clown, pumpkin head, zombie, or witch).
- We do **not** require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use the App.
- We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your photos for advertising.
- We do **not** show ads, and we do **not** use third-party analytics or tracking. We only use Apple's built-in crash reporting (App Store Connect).
- Apple handles all payments. We never see or store your card details.
- Before your first photo is uploaded, the App asks for your permission to send it to fal.ai.
- Photos and Generated Images at fal.ai expire and are deleted automatically within 24 hours.
- The App is rated **13+**.

## 2. Information we process

### 2.1 Photos you choose (including your face)
When you take a selfie or pick a photo from your library, the App sends that photo to fal.ai so its AI model (Flux Kontext) can generate your costume image. The photo shows your face, so we treat it as sensitive personal information.

We use your photo **only** to create the costume image you asked for. We do **not**:
- use your photo to identify you or recognize your face;
- create face templates, faceprints, or other biometric identifiers from it;
- use your photo to train AI models, or let anyone else do so on our behalf;
- use your photo for advertising, profiling, or marketing;
- post or publish your photo anywhere.

The App only gets access to photos you choose. If you use the photo library picker, the App sees only the photos you select. Camera access is used only when you take a picture in the App. You can turn camera or photo access off at any time in iOS **Settings → Halloween Costume AI Cam**.

### 2.2 Generated images
The costume images the AI makes from your photo ("Generated Images") are sent back to your device. They are shown in the App and saved to your Photos library only if you choose to save or share them.

### 2.3 Purchase information
The App sells in-app credit packs (10, 30, or 100 generations) through Apple's In-App Purchase. **Apple processes the payment.** We do not get or store your name, payment card number, or billing address. Apple tells the App that a purchase went through so we can add your credits. Your credit balance and a record of your free first reveal are stored on your device. Apple's handling of your payment information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

### 2.4 Crash reports
If the App crashes, Apple's built-in crash reporting (App Store Connect) may collect technical details such as device model, iOS version, App version, and what the App was doing when it crashed. We use this only to find and fix bugs. Crash reports do not include your photos, and we do not use them to identify you or track you across other apps or websites.

### 2.5 What we do NOT collect
We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, browsing history, advertising identifier (IDFA), or any account login, because there are no accounts.

If you email us for support, we will get your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use that only to reply to you.

## 3. Who we share information with

### 3.1 fal.ai (AI processing provider / subprocessor)
We send your selected photo, and the costume style you chose, to **fal.ai** (Features & Labels, Inc.). fal.ai runs the AI model and sends the Generated Image back. fal.ai processes this data for us so it can provide the service. fal.ai's privacy policy: https://fal.ai/privacy

The App asks for your permission before the first time it sends any photo to fal.ai. If you say no, no photo is uploaded, and the App cannot make costume images.

### 3.2 Apple
Apple processes In-App Purchases and may give us crash reports that you allowed under your device's "Share with App Developers" setting, via Apple's built-in crash reporting (App Store Connect).

### 3.3 Legal reasons
We may share information if the law requires it, for example to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users, the public, or us.

### 3.4 No sale or "sharing" of personal information
We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law. We do not use your data to track you.

## 4. How long we keep information (retention and deletion)

- **On our side:** We do not run our own database of your photos. We do not keep copies of your selfies or Generated Images on our servers.
- **At fal.ai:** Photos you upload and Generated Images are kept by fal.ai temporarily and expire and are deleted automatically within 24 hours. We configure this limit on every request, for both the uploaded photo and the generated image. fal.ai may keep limited technical logs (for example request IDs and timestamps) under its own policies.
- **On your device:** Your credit balance, free-reveal status, App settings, and any images you saved stay on your iPhone. Images you saved to your Photos library stay there until you delete them. Deleting the App removes the App's local data, except images you saved to Photos.
- **Crash reports:** These are kept only as long as needed to fix problems, under Apple's App Store Connect crash reporting retention rules.
- **Support emails:** These are kept only as long as needed to help you and keep basic records.

## 5. Children

The App is rated **13+**. It is **not directed at children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information, including photos, from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App to upload a photo, contact us at ege.bakici35@gmail.com and we will help get it deleted. Please do not upload photos of children unless you are their parent or legal guardian and consent to it.

## 6. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California (CCPA/CPRA), the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR), you may have the right to:

- **Know / access:** ask what personal information we have about you;
- **Delete:** ask us to delete your personal information;
- **Correct:** ask us to fix inaccurate information;
- **Object or restrict:** object to or limit certain processing;
- **Portability:** get a copy of your information in a usable format;
- **Withdraw consent:** stop us from sending any more photos to fal.ai. To do this, stop generating images, or remove camera and photo access in iOS Settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened;
- **Not be discriminated against** for using these rights.

Because we have no accounts and do not keep your photos on our own servers, we usually hold very little or nothing that can be linked to you. To make a request, email ege.bakici35@gmail.com. We may need to ask you some questions to handle your request, and we will reply within the time the law requires (generally 30 days under GDPR and 45 days under CCPA).

**Legal bases (EEA/UK users):** We process your photo based on your **explicit consent**, which you give before upload, and to **perform our contract** with you, which is to provide the costume images you asked for. We process crash reports based on our **legitimate interest** in keeping the App working. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

**International transfers:** fal.ai and our providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 7. Security

Photos are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of sending or storing data is 100% secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect your information.
